# Scientific scope review recorded before heldout truth access

This is a computationally executed exploratory evaluation,not a confirmation of
methodological superiority. No analysis rule is changed by this review.

- The20declared scenario families(14evaluation units)are not40independently
  authored mechanisms. New combinations/evidence packets reuse familiar causal
  motifs. In particular,the compatible-world family extends a previously seen
  equivalence-witness core with ancillary measurements;it is not independent
  evidence of generalization to a wholly new identification principle. Do not
  relabel the locked84card/14cluster comparison as externally independent novelty.
- The deterministic baseline knows all6document grammar styles. It is a strong
  author-built evidence compiler,not a generic parser trained on unrelated prose.
  Its advantage cannot be interpreted as universal performance against language
  models. Models receive the same information,but source-ID rubric matching can
  still penalize a semantically defensible citation;independent adjudication is
  absent. The benchmark tests constrained evidence use,not medical truth discovery.
- With the locked veto rule,the critic cannot remove any graph already admitted
  by the exact compiler. Screening-plus-critic and screening alone are redundant
  by construction. Critic citation/verdict fidelity is still evaluated,but no
  incremental downstream critic benefit can be claimed from this treatment.
- Binary generators have limited functional complexity. Many nuisance functions
  are simple within treatment arms. This is not validation across arbitrary
  nonlinear/high-dimensional clinical mechanisms,proxy identification,front-door,
  instrumental-variable or death-defined estimands.
-14fixed family units and sparse errors do not validate bootstrap coverage or
  confirmatory risk precision. Prior severe undercoverage remains unresolved.
  Report achieved assertion rates,unknown-certificate bounds and every failure.
- The provider-slug error is an investigator pipeline error,not a model reasoning
  failure. Preserve all initial attempts,corrected one-attempt-per-slot requests,
  unchanged calibration and both pre-truth prediction versions. Different
  collection concurrency cohorts preclude equivalent operational comparisons.
- SHARE associations remain observational;timing,confounding,selection,death and
  unavailable longitudinal survey weights block an unconditional intervention
  conclusion. No amount of LLM agreement repairs those missing assumptions.

These limitations restrict scientific claims and require substantive human review
before submission. They do not justify dropping unfavorable methods or altering
the existing experimental predictions. Code/synthetic reproduction is distinct
from clinical validation or a guarantee of journal suitability.
