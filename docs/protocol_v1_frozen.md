# Approved protocol v1 — internal freeze, 2026-10-16

**PI decision recorded:** The principal research contribution is identification-aware causal conclusion reliability and principled abstention. The SHARE 7–9 application is primarily **observational**, with no unconditional intervention effect asserted. Childhood financial circumstance effect modification is exploratory, and Wave 8 EURO-D is an ambiguous-time **sensitivity** variable, not a default baseline adjustment. The controlling specification remains `docs/MASTER_PROMPT_v2.md`.

**Scope of freeze:** This locks the *next non-oracle evaluation* and methodological decision rules. It is **not external preregistration**. Earlier synthetic results, real SHARE estimates and initial smoke tests were seen before this freeze, making the overall study exploratory and creating potential investigator degrees of freedom. We explicitly reserve disjoint numeric heldout seeds and new mechanism families for the first internal challenge, but do not claim this is a fully independent replication. Later protocol changes require a dated amendment and separate output location; past results will not be overwritten.

## Primary scientific question and estimand

**Methodological estimand:** Among known-truth synthetic tasks drawn from the prespecified mechanisms and seed distribution, compare the conditional probability of a **wrong or materially unjustified treatment-effect sign** among tasks where each method makes a directional assertion, with assertion/coverage rates shown alongside. An asserted positive/negative direction is scored wrong when the finite-sample true ATE has the opposite sign or absolute magnitude ≤0.10 synthetic outcome units. A claim made when the true data-generating graph has uncontrolled latent common causes or informative outcome selection is separately counted as an *identification error*, even if its sign happens to be correct.

**Primary validation:** Each method may declare a directional sign only when its prespecified interval or graph-supported robustness envelope excludes zero, and calibration can further suppress but never create assertions. Apply development-only thresholds targeting **50% assertion coverage**. A fair risk comparison is possible only when heldout assertion rates differ from 50% by ≤10 percentage points; otherwise label the arms not coverage-matched rather than interpreting their conditional risks as superiority. Always show full observed risk/coverage trade-offs at locked margin thresholds 0, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50; methods that never assert have **undefined risk** and are not successes.

**Data separation:** `benchmark/blind.py` receives a public variable/timing card and an observable DataFrame **without generator labels, graph truth, potential outcomes, true ATE, or mechanism family**. Its offline graph sources are deterministic fixtures **not LLM responses**. `benchmark/nonoracle.py` has access to ground truth *only after* `analyze_blind` returns. The graph candidates are identical across all mechanism families and seeds. Later real LLM source proposals will use the same public-information boundary and require independent prospective evaluation; they are not part of this frozen offline experiment.

**Graph support:** Hard scientific constraints reject cycles and arrows from known post-exposure M to exposure A or post-outcome C to earlier events. Do not equate voting frequency with a causal edge probability. Among temporally valid, provenance-bearing candidate graphs, enumerate observed-variable backdoor adjustment sets. The deterministic single graph, naive majority, separate valid/invalid graph fixtures and screened robust set are compared, alongside unadjusted/all-adjustment baselines and strict unmeasured-U abstention. Only compatible assumed **total effects** are combined in a graph/scenario envelope. Unsupported/nonidentified graphs do not yield a numeric causal effect estimate.

**Conditional uncertainty rule:** Given precommitted candidate graphs, report an all-scenarios sign only if each identifies the total effect with an admissible measured-pre-exposure adjustment set, and the union of *model-conditional* intervals shifted by **−0.25, 0, +0.25** synthetic outcome units stays strictly on one side of zero. Bias shifts are arbitrary stress-test parameters, NOT calibrated bounds on hidden confounding. Outcome missingness triggers abstention under the conservative selection rule; separate unmeasured-U stress inclusion deliberately forces abstention and tests assertion-cost tradeoffs. The graph envelope **is not a simultaneous 95% confidence set**.

**Synthetic holdout:** Five new mechanism classes in `benchmark/challenge.py`: threshold nonlinear outcome/mediator, hidden confounding with proxy, informative outcome loss, heterogeneous near-zero ATE, and weak overlap. Both positive and negative generating effects appear through seed parity; the near-zero mechanism challenges facile sign claims. Use n=800, **eight development seeds 1101–1108**, and **12 heldout seeds 22001–22012**, independent of the previous 2026-10-15 test seeds. These are not calibrated using SHARE coefficients or respondent distributions. Parameters and mechanism source code are frozen before the heldout run.

**Reporting:** Show all 5×12 heldout replications if execution succeeds; denominators, failure logs, false signs, nonidentified assertions, point-estimate RMSE, conditional interval coverage, abstention and graph-screen audit. Include development split only for threshold calibration; do not optimize code or thresholds on heldout risk after observing it. Require uncertainty around cross-replicate estimates before any claim of superiority; multiple mechanisms and only 12 seeds/family make inference preliminary. Keep older oracle-guided benchmarks explicitly separate, never pool them with this result as comparable arms.

## SHARE descriptive application

Eligible W8 interviewees with valid `ac035d5` linked to W9 outcome; evaluate observed EURO-D conditional on follow-up observation. Main outputs: crude difference, age/sex/education/country standardized association (cross-fitted AIPW *as a model-standardization estimator*, **not identified causal ATE**), and W8 depression inclusion only as time-ambiguous sensitivity. Report selection/death, country, positivity, childhood category coverage. Under reasonable alternative graphs with U and outcome-survival selection, **abstain** from unconditional causal effect claims. Treat estimates computed before approval as **exploratory**; their confidence intervals only express model-conditional sampling variation, not uncertainty over structural assumptions.

## Publication gate and change control

Novelty remains unresolved relative to literature on DAG uncertainty/bounds and LLM causal graph constraints; no claim of new-method performance is accepted without real 3–4 model trials, baselines, and independently replicated known-truth tests. OpenRouter live calls prohibited absent *separate explicit PI authorization*; require runtime opt-in and budget controls. `RUN_STATUS.md` must show exit codes, checksums and all dev/heldout run failures. No SHARE raw or respondent-level artifacts, access tokens or real-data private results may enter the public GitHub repository. A human reviews permissible aggregate disclosures and causal graphs before publication.
